##### 6/6/26

### Final presentation

---

For my final blog, it was time to present and give an elevator pitch on my freedom project. Both my elevator pitch and presentation were still a bit nervewracking for me, but I learned a lot from them. With my [presentation](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/1-FdZwCkM-2yuI1CBX0Z7Xu43Bxr-k9O-Xo6iGmgypHg/edit?slide=id.g3a8611da9a0_0_0#slide=id.g3a8611da9a0_0_0), I was very proud of how I explained how I used Vue.js and the many useful factors of my tool. However, as a presenter, I would like to continue to raise the volume of my voice and also make more/prolonged eye contact with everyone (instead of just one side). Besides that, I strongly believe the visuals on my slides were clearly readable, and I was able to deliver all the points I had across. Regarding my [elevator pitch](https://github.com/joleel3389/sep11-freedom-project/blob/main/prep/pitch.md), I took away that for the future, I'd like to add more details that more clearly distinguish the creativity of my website. After doing my elevator pitch, I felt like there were more details I wanted to add to make my project stand out compared to similar projects (such as Quizlet, etc.), which I didn't get the chance to do. On the bright side, I felt very confident when answering questions from the judges and felt my project was very practical. Overall, I definitely improved my elevator pitch this year compared to the previous year (SEP10) and I'm very satisfied.

### Engineering Design Process

---

In my previous blog, I was on the 5th and 6th steps of EDP, but I'm currently on the 7th step. For the 7th step, I'm going to be improving my presentation skills as needed, as I still think there are some areas for me to improve on. ### Skills

---

Below are the skills I've learned while working on my blog.
* Communication
    * When giving my presentation, I still feel like my public speaking needs some work in an aspect where my voice isn't projected loud enough. Without a microphone, I don't have good control over my breath and tend to feel out of breath when I attempt to raise my voice for prolonged periods of time. During my presentation, I was able to use a microphone since the AC was on, but I won't always get this opportunity. I want to be able to work on my breath control and volume of my voice.
* Consideration
    * When making my presentation slides, I had to be considerate of how they were portrayed. This meant that I made sure people could read the text and images on my slide. If my text or images weren't big enough, this would make it harder for people sitting far away to read my slides. So to accomplish this, I made the font sizes big enough and simplified some code (so it'd all fit).

### Next steps

---

For my next steps, I'd like to move on to step 8: to communicate the results. I would like to continue to increase the pitch of my voice as I prepare to present in the SEP showcase!
