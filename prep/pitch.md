# Pitch

## Tool: Vue.js
## Product: Calculus memorization mini-game

---

## Main pitch (1 min max)

Hi! Do you like math? Well whether or not you like math, my project is made to be useful to help your strengthen or grow your math skills, especially in AP calculus. Here's my website using Vue.js. It's simple on the eyes so users are engaged in the active memorization content. There are 3 decks, 'unit circle', 'derivatives', and 'identities'. When you click a deck, there's a score and progress check up top, with questions below. If you get a question wrong, a sad sprite will show and 0.5 points are deducted. If you get it right, a cheeky sprite will show. You have infinite attempts to answer, aimed to help users memorize the right answer for the future. Anytime you want to exit a deck, just click on 'home' in the navbar. Do you have any questions?
